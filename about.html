Our Story & Passion

The Heart Behind Cookies and Cream

Born out of an obsession for authentic taste, velvety chocolate, and genuine hospitality. We set out to give Nizamabad a cozy sanctuary where sweet moments are crafted every day.

Welcome to CNC

Where Flavor Meets Cozy Hospitality

Located in Srinagar Colony, Vinayak Nagar, COOKIES AND CREAM (CNC) was envisioned as more than just a typical cafe. It is a warm haven where friends gather for endless laughs, families celebrate special milestones, and dessert enthusiasts discover their next sweet addiction.

Whether it’s the satisfying crunch of crushed cookies inside our signature thick shake, the rich aroma of freshly pulled Arabica espresso, or the hot sizzle of our chocolate walnut brownies, every item on our handcrafted menu is prepared with meticulous care and uncompromised quality.

Our deep dark green and warm gold aesthetic was thoughtfully chosen to reflect relaxation, luxury, and tranquility—a retreat away from the busy streets of the city.

Our Guiding Philosophy

The Three Pillars of Our Kitchen

What sets Cookies and Cream apart in every plate, glass, and cup we serve.

Artisan Sourcing

We source authentic cocoa, pure vanilla, 100% Arabica plantation beans, fresh dairy cream, and crisp farm vegetables. No artificial compromises.

Made Fresh to Order

Every burger patty is grilled hot, every waffle is baked fresh upon order, and every pizza is hand-stretched and baked until the cheese turns bubbly golden.

Heartfelt Hospitality

We believe every guest deserves to be greeted with a warm smile. Our team takes pride in making you feel relaxed, whether you stay 20 minutes or 3 hours.
